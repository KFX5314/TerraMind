### Example: UN R150 paragraph 4.1.1
SOURCE: Retro-reflective devices shall be so constructed that they function satisfactorily and will continue to do so in normal use. In addition, they must not have any defect in design or manufacture that is detrimental to their efficient operation or to their maintenance in good condition.
REQUIREMENTS:
- The retro-reflective devices shall be so constructed that they function satisfactorily and will continue to do so in normal use.
- The retro-reflective devices shall not have any defect in design or manufacture that is detrimental to their efficient operation or to their maintenance in good condition.

### Example: UN R150 paragraph 5.1.1
SOURCE: Every retro-reflector of the Classes IA and IB, when tested according to paragraph 5.1.7., shall meet: (a) The dimensions and shape requirements set forth in Annex 5; and (b) The photometric and colorimetric requirements as specified in paragraphs 5.1.4. to 5.1.5.; and (c) The physical and mechanical requirements set forth in paragraph 5.1.7., depending on the nature of the materials and construction of the retro-reflective devices.
REQUIREMENTS:
- When tested according to paragraph 5.1.7., every retro-reflector of the Classes IA and IB shall meet the dimensions and shape requirements set forth in Annex 5.
- When tested according to paragraph 5.1.7., every retro-reflector of the Classes IA and IB shall meet the photometric and colorimetric requirements as specified in paragraphs 5.1.4. to 5.1.5.
- When tested according to paragraph 5.1.7., every retro-reflector of the Classes IA and IB shall meet the physical and mechanical requirements set forth in paragraph 5.1.7., depending on the nature of the materials and construction of the retro-reflective devices.

### Example: UN R150 paragraph 3.3.1.2
SOURCE: All markings shall be clearly legible and indelible.
REQUIREMENTS:
- The markings shall be clearly legible and indelible.

### Example: UN R150 paragraph 4.1.4
SOURCE: The outer surface of retro-reflective devices shall be easy to clean. The surface shall therefore not be rough and any protuberances they may exhibit shall not prevent easy cleaning.
REQUIREMENTS:
- The outer surface of retro-reflective devices shall be easy to clean.
- The outer surface of retro-reflective devices shall not be rough and any protuberances the outer surface of retro-reflective devices may exhibit shall not prevent easy cleaning.

### Example: UN R10 paragraph 5.2.2
SOURCE: Electrical/electronic sub-assembly (ESA) An approval mark described in paragraph 5.3. below shall be affixed to every ESA conforming to a type approved under this Regulation. No marking is required for electrical/electronic systems built into vehicles which are approved as units.
REQUIREMENTS:
- The electrical/electronic sub-assembly (ESA) conforming to a type approved under this Regulation shall have an approval mark described in paragraph 5.3. affixed to it, except that no marking is required for electrical/electronic systems built into vehicles which are approved as units.
